## A Reminder of the Evoluationary Context That Built the Strategies Governing Early Development

Only metazoans (animals) go through gastrulation.

## The diploblastic animals

- cnidarians: jellyfish, hydra
- ctenophores: comb jelly
- generally *radial symmetry*

### The triploblastic animals

- the evolution of mesoderm - muscles + circulatory system - allowed mobility and larger bodies
- generally *bilateral symmetry*

#### Protostome

"mouth first"

- schizocoely "hollowing out" of previously solid cord of mesodermal cells to form body cavity/coelom

- ecdysozoan are animals that shed ("ecdysis" Greek for "get out of") / molt
- Arthropoda phylum: includes insects, arachnids, mites, etc.

- lophotrochozoans have a common spiral cleavage and larval form (trochophores)
- 14 / 35 metazoan phyla, including flatworms, annelids, mollusks
- spiral cleavage endows 'spiralia'

#### Deuterostomes

- "mouth second"; oral opening formed after anal opening
- basically chordates and echinoderms (sea stars, sea urchines)
- recall chordates includes vertebrates but also invertebrates turnicates (sea squirts) and lancelets
