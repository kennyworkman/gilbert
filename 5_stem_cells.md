Stem Cells
===

Cell maturation as:
Specification -> commitment -> differentiation

Driven by tools granted in cell-cell communication and differential gene expression.

Stem cell can basically recreate itself while also generating specialized
progeny. There are many types and really just refers to ability to resist
differentiating into a tissue's postmitotic derivatives.

## "The Stem Cell Concept"

- produce replica of itself, *self-renewal*
- make a daughter cell that undergoes further development

The split occurs in two broad patterns:

- single cell asymmetry
- population asymmetry

### Potency

- totipotent
- pluripotent
- multipotent

Most adult stem cells are multipotent, with not only restricted differentiation
potential but limited number of self-renewal rounds (aging).

Daughter cells often become *progenitors*/*transit-amplifying*, a specific term
in between stem and daughter. Often serves to increase number in pool before
final differentiation.

## Stem Cell Regulation

Extracellular:
- Physical: cell-cell or cell-matrix adhesion or cell density create mechanical forces
- Chemical: endocrine, juxtacrine, paracrine

Intracellular:
- Partitioning items in cytoplasm
- Transcriptional regulation
- Epigenetic regulation
